AIML Summer Internship 2026 — Capstone Project 2
IIHMF, MNNIT Allahabad, Prayagraj

## Objective
Estimate house prices across 24 Prayagraj localities from area, rooms, building age,
locality and amenities, and present them in a map-based Streamlit app with a budget
view, property comparison, EMI calculator and data insights.

> **The data is synthetic.** Prices are calibrated to mid-2026 listing rates from
> 99acres / Housing.com locality pages, but every house row is generated by
> `Dataset/generate_dataset.py`. Treat all prices as illustrations, not market values
> or valuations.

## App Features
- **Map** of all localities, coloured live by your budget (within / stretch / over), with famous places
- **Rooms from area** — bedrooms and bathrooms are suggested from the area. Rooms you set
  yourself are kept when you change the area (↺ button returns to the suggestion)
- **Reveal my estimate** — freezes the estimate for the inputs you pressed it with; if you
  change inputs afterwards the app says so until you press the button again
- **What can my budget buy?** — largest home your budget gets in your locality, and what it
  gets in every other locality (useful when your own locality is out of reach)
- **Download all-locality estimates** — CSV of the estimate for every locality with your inputs
- **Compare** two properties side by side with the main reasons for the price difference
- **EMI calculator** — price comes from your estimate, down payment defaults to 20% of the price
- **Insights** — price distribution, rate per locality, correlation heatmap
- Sizes below 300 sq ft are rejected (nothing liveable fits)

## Model
- **Features:** `area_sqft, bedrooms, bathrooms, age_years, distance_to_city_km, total_rooms,
  amenity_score, is_new, garage, garden, lift, near_school, location`
- **Target:** `price` in rupees. Models learn `log(price)` and predictions are converted
  back, because prices run from about ₹12 lakh to ₹15 crore.
- **Monotonic constraint:** the deployed XGBoost model is constrained so that price can never
  go *up* as a building gets older (`age_years` decreasing, `is_new` increasing). The
  training script checks this on 150 homes and stops if it fails.
- **Export:** XGBoost is saved with `save_model` as JSON, which loads across XGBoost versions
  (no pickle, no version warning). Scaling constants and locality encoding live in
  `model_metadata.json`, so the app needs only `xgboost` to predict.
- **Trained range:** 350 to 12,000 sq ft, up to 13 bedrooms and 10 bathrooms. The app limits
  rooms to that range. Above 12,000 sq ft the price is scaled with area and flagged as a rough guide.

## Data Preparation
Rows without `area_sqft` are dropped (a median would invent a size), `bathrooms` and
`garage` gaps are median-filled, duplicates are removed, and rows whose price per sq ft is
implausible for their locality are removed (IQR rule, k=3, on the log of rate / locality
median). Large homes are kept on purpose so the model learns big plots and many rooms.
